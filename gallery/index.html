About

Photos from Our Casablanca Valley Wine Tours

Welcome to our photo gallery. These are real pictures from our tours: the vineyards, cellars and colourful streets that make Chile's wine country and Valparaíso so easy to fall for.

We've been guiding travellers since 2007 through the Casablanca Valley, and on custom tours through the Maipo Valley and Colchagua Valley too, as well as around the historic port city of Valparaíso. Here's a taste of what you'll find:

  • Vineyards in the Chilean sun, from coastal rows of vines to the hills behind them
  • Wine tastings with boutique family producers and well-known wineries
  • Wineries you'll spot: Bodegas RE, Catrala, Loma Larga, Quintay, Emiliana, Casas del Bosque, Lapostolle and Montes
  • Behind the scenes in the cellar, with barrels being topped up and bottles labelled by hand
  • Valparaíso and Viña del Mar, with street art, steep hills and plenty of heritage
  • Our guests, enjoying a relaxed day out in a small group

Every photo was taken on one of our tours, so what you see here is what you'll get. If it makes you want to come along, take a look at our Casablanca Valley wine tours, or get in touch and we'll plan a route around you, including Maipo or Colchagua.
