# Wine Tours Valparaíso

> Guided wine tours to Chile's Casablanca Valley, with hotel pickup from Valparaíso, Viña del Mar and Santiago, and collection from the Valparaíso and San Antonio cruise ports.

Wine Tours Valparaíso is a small tour operator based in Valparaíso, Chile, running guided tours to the Casablanca Valley — a cool-climate wine region between Santiago and the Pacific coast, known for Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Syrah. Tours visit the Bodegas RE and Casas del Bosque wineries and include transport with a driver and guide, winery entrance fees, and tours and tastings at both estates. The company also runs city tours of Valparaíso and Viña del Mar.

Tours are conducted in English and Spanish. Private tours and custom itineraries are available, and larger groups can be accommodated with advance booking.

## Practical details

- Departure points: hotels in Valparaíso, Viña del Mar and Santiago.
- Cruise passengers: pickup and drop-off at the Valparaíso cruise terminal and the port of San Antonio. Valparaíso pricing is shown in the site's price calculator; San Antonio is quoted on request.
- Wineries visited on tours: Bodegas RE and Casas del Bosque, both in the Casablanca Valley.
- Booking: by enquiry through the website contact form, email or WhatsApp — there is no instant online checkout. At least 24 hours' notice is required.
- Payment: major credit cards, PayPal, and cash in Chilean pesos.
- Cancellation: full refund with 48 hours' notice; partial refund after that.
- Transport with a driver and guide is included in all tours.
- Children are welcome; tastings are for adults only.
