Where to Stay in Valparaíso — Areas Picked by a Local Guide

We've been guiding tours out of Valparaíso since 2007, which means we have picked up guests from most of the hotels below more times than we can count. This isn't a paid list — it's the hotels we'd point a friend toward.

Most of the best boutique hotels cluster in Cerro Alegre and Cerro Concepción, the two hills above the port that give Valparaíso its colorful, art-covered reputation — walkable, close to restaurants and galleries, and steep enough that a funicular (ascensor) or a short taxi ride helps at the end of the day.

Every property on this list can be a pickup point for our wine and city tours — just tell us where you're staying when you book.
