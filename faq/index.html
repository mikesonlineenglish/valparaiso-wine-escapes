Tours & itineraries

What regions do you cover?

Our standard tours run in the Casablanca Valley, plus city tours in Valparaíso and Viña del Mar. We also arrange custom tours in Maipo, Aconcagua and Colchagua.

Do you offer private tours?

Yes, we specialize in small, personalized tours tailored to your interests.

Can you arrange group tours?

Absolutely, we can accommodate larger groups with advance booking.

What is included in a wine tour?

Transport, guided visits to wineries, tastings, and cultural insights. Lunch on arrangement.

Are meals included?

Some tours include lunch at a winery or local restaurant; details vary by package.

Do you customize itineraries?

Yes, we tailor tours to your preferences, whether wine-focused or cultural.

Do you offer city tours?

Yes, we provide tours around Valparaíso and Viña del Mar, highlighting history and culture.

Do you offer multi-day packages?

Yes, we can arrange extended itineraries across multiple valleys.

Prices & booking

How do I book a tour?

Through our website, email, or by contacting us directly.

Do I need to book in advance? How far ahead?

At least 24 hours.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and cash in Chilean pesos.

What is your cancellation policy?

Full refunds are available with 48 hours' notice; partial refunds after that.

Getting there & pickup

Do you pick up from Santiago?

Yes, tours can start from Santiago, Valparaíso, or Viña del Mar.

Do you offer shore excursions from the Valparaíso and San Antonio cruise ports?

Yes — we collect and drop off at both the Valparaíso cruise terminal and the port of San Antonio. Pricing for Valparaíso pickup is shown in our price calculator; for San Antonio, contact us and we'll quote you directly. Every tour page lists its full duration, so you can match a half-day or full-day tour to the time your ship is in port.

Is transport included?

Comfortable private transport is included in all tours.

Practical

What languages are tours offered in?

Tours are conducted in English and Spanish.

Can children join wine tours?

Children are welcome, though tastings are reserved for adults.

How can I contact you?

Email: info@winetoursvalparaiso.com or via our website contact form. You can also WhatsApp us.

About us

How long has Wine Tours Valparaíso been operating?

Since 2007, with nearly two decades of experience guiding visitors.

Who is the owner?

British expat Michael Ayandokun, who studied Latin American culture and has lived in Chile for over 20 years.

Are your guides certified?

Yes, Michael has a Chilean School of Sommeliers certificate.

What makes your tours unique?

Personal attention, cultural depth, and authentic experiences beyond mass tourism.
